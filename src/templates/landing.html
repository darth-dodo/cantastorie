How it works

A bedtime app that winds a child down.

Two taps to a story

A tap wakes the shelf and it greets your child aloud. Tap a cover; pages turn themselves.

Choices are pictures

At a fork, two picture cards appear with spoken labels. A finger picks the path.

One warm narrator

One storyteller voice across every language. Every prompt is spoken.

Private by design

Nothing about your child leaves the device. No child accounts, no child tracking. Every story needs your approval before it reaches a shelf.

Native, never translated

Five languages

Italian and Spanish lead; English, Greek, and German ride along.
